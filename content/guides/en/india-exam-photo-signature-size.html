Almost every recruitment and entrance portal in India rejects a photo or signature that is a few kilobytes off. The rules also differ between exams: RRB wants a signature of 30–49KB, SSC wants 10–20KB, UPSC wants a 20–200KB photo and one image with three signatures. This page collects the current numbers from each exam's official notice, explains what they mean, and links each one to a tool preset that produces a file that passes.

Numbers change between notifications. Everything below is taken from the notices named in each section (checked October 2026; UPSC re-checked November 2026). If your notice says something different, follow your notice.

RRB NTPC (CEN 06/2026)

The Railway Recruitment Boards changed the process in their recent notices: the photograph is captured live with your webcam or phone camera during the application, and it is checked against your photo ID. You must not photograph an existing printed or digital photo — the notice says such applications are rejected.

The signature is still uploaded. Paragraph 14.5.1 of CEN 06/2026 sets these rules:

  • JPG/JPEG only, file size between 30KB and 49KB.
  • At least 140 pixels wide and 60 pixels high, scanned at a minimum of 100 DPI.
  • Black ink on white paper, in running (joined) handwriting — not block, capital or disjointed letters.
  • The signature sits inside a 35 mm × 20 mm box and must be fully visible.

The 30KB minimum surprises people: a clean signature on white paper is a very simple image and usually compresses to well under 30KB. Re-saving it at a huge size to gain kilobytes makes the scan look odd. The RRB signature preset saves a 280 × 160 px JPG (the shape of the 35 × 20 mm box) at the best quality that fits under 49KB, and if the file is still below 30KB it adds inert padding so the size check passes while the picture stays the same.

Rejection reasons the notice lists include a non-white background, non-black ink, capital letters, an unclear or partly visible signature, and a thumb impression in place of a signature.

SSC (CGL, CHSL and other notices)

SSC also captures the photograph live while you fill in the form, and asks you to look the same on exam day as in that photo. The notices warn that capturing a photo of an existing photograph leads to rejection.

The signature is a scanned JPG/JPEG of 10–20KB. The CGL and CHSL notices give the image dimension as about 6.0 cm (width) × 2.0 cm (height); the One Time Registration instructions mention about 4.0 × 2.0 cm. Both describe a wide, short signature. SSC says blurred or miniature signatures are rejected, so fill the image with the signature rather than leaving a small scribble in a big white area. Use the SSC signature preset (300 × 100 px, 10–20KB). The rejection reasons and the 80% rule are in the SSC signature and photo guide.

UPSC (upsconline.nic.in)

UPSC moved applications to a new portal, upsconline.nic.in, in 2025, and its upload rules are not the ones most websites still quote. Its photo and signature instructions (February 2026) say:

  • Photo: a colour JPG between 20KB and 200KB, saved with the file name photo. Your face must cover at least 75% of the photo, looking straight at the camera with both ears showing, on a plain white background. UPSC's own samples refuse an ordinary passport photo with shoulders.
  • Signature: sign three times, one below the other, in black ink on plain white paper, and put all three in one JPG of 20KB to 100KB and 350–500 pixels, saved as signature.
  • You also take a live photo in the form, and it is matched with the uploaded one.

The old 20–300KB limit, the 350–1000 pixel range and the "name and date on the photo, not older than 10 days" rule belonged to the previous portal. Presets: UPSC photo (450 × 600 px, 20–200KB, saved as photo.jpg) and UPSC signature (500 × 500 px, 20–100KB, saved as signature.jpg). The full list of rules, the reject samples and the 2027 dates are in the UPSC photo and signature guide.

NEET (UG) 2026

The NEET (UG) 2026 information bulletin from NTA lists these uploads:

  • Passport-size photograph: JPG between 10KB and 200KB, colour or black and white, against a white background, with about 80% of the image being your face (without a mask) and your ears visible.
  • Signature: JPG between 10KB and 100KB.
  • Left and right hand finger and thumb impressions: JPG between 10KB and 200KB.
  • Identity proof: 50KB to 300KB.

NTA has also asked for a postcard-size (4 × 6 inch) photograph in recent years; check the bulletin for your year. Presets: passport photo under 200KB and NEET signature, 10–100KB.

UGC NET (December 2026)

The UGC-NET December 2026 information bulletin (applications 8–28 October 2026) asks for a live photograph taken in the form, an uploaded photograph of 10–200KB with 80% of the face visible including the ears against a white background, and a signature of 10–50KB. Many sites still show the older NTA signature limit of 4–30KB; a very clean signature made to that rule can fall under the new 10KB minimum. Details, dates and the live photo step: UGC NET photo and signature size. Presets: passport photo under 200KB and UGC NET signature, 10–50KB.

IBPS and SBI bank exams

Bank exams have the most uploads: photo 200 × 230 px (20–50KB), signature 140 × 60 px (10–20KB), left thumb impression 240 × 240 px (20–50KB) and a handwritten declaration 800 × 400 px (50–100KB). The bank exam documents page has a preset and an example for each, plus the declaration text.

Mistakes that get uploads rejected

  1. Capital letters. RRB and the bank exams reject signatures in BLOCK or CAPITAL letters. Sign the way you normally sign.
  2. Blue or coloured ink, lined paper. Use a black pen on plain white paper; ruled lines and a grey background look like a bad scan.
  3. A tiny signature in a large blank image. Crop close to the signature so it fills the frame.
  4. Wrong KB. Both too big and too small are rejected when a range is given. Check the size shown after saving.
  5. Photographing a photo. For live-capture portals (RRB, SSC) the photo must be of you, taken at that moment.
  6. A passport photo with shoulders for UPSC. The UPSC portal wants your face to cover three quarters of the photo. Crop from the top of your hair to just below your chin.

How to make the signature file

  1. Sign on plain white paper with a black pen, two or three times, and pick the best one. (UPSC is different: it wants all three signatures in one image.)
  2. Photograph it from straight above in daylight, without your phone's shadow on the paper. (Or draw it with your finger on the signature page.)
  3. Open the preset for your exam from the links above. The paper is cleaned to white, the signature is cropped and centred, and the file is saved as a JPG inside the KB range.
  4. Check the pixel size and KB shown under the result, then upload.

If your exam is not listed, copy the size rules from your notice and paste them into the Paste your form's requirements box on the signature or photo page; the sizes and KB range are filled in for you. Certificates and ID proofs as PDF can be shrunk to an exact limit with compress PDF to 100KB or 200KB.
