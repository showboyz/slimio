About SlimIO

SlimIO is a set of free tools for the files that application forms and inboxes keep rejecting: PDFs that are too big, photos that are the wrong size, signatures that need to be a JPG of exactly 10–20KB. You can compress a PDF (even to an exact size like 100KB), merge, split, remove, rotate and reorder pages, add page numbers or a watermark, sign a PDF or stamp it with a seal, convert between PDF and JPG, and resize photos, ID photos and signatures to the pixels and kilobytes a form asks for. The tools work in English and Korean.

Why it exists

Job portals that accept only files under 100KB, exam forms that want a 140 × 60 pixel signature between 10KB and 20KB, an email that bounces because the attachment is too large — these are everyday problems, and they shouldn't require an account, a watermark on your document, or sending personal papers to a service you know nothing about. SlimIO started as a way to make those small jobs quick and private, and grew one tool at a time as people ran into the next limit.

Who makes it

SlimIO is built and run by one independent developer. There is no company behind it and no investors; the site is kept free by a daily usage limit and small ads that may be shown on the pages. Feedback goes straight to the person who writes the code — write in if something doesn't work or a tool is missing.

How your files are handled

  • In your browser. Merging, splitting, rotating, page numbers, watermarks, signing, PDF↔JPG, photo and signature resizing all run on your own device. The files are never uploaded.
  • On the server, briefly. The default "sharp" PDF compression sends the file over HTTPS to our server, where Ghostscript compresses it; the file is deleted as soon as the result is sent back. Nothing is kept, and no one looks at it. If a file must not leave your device, choose the private mode.
  • No account, no watermark. Open a page and use it. The daily limit is counted per device, not per person.

Details are in the privacy policy.

How we check the numbers

Presets like "RRB signature 30–49KB" or "Q-Net photo 300 × 400" come from the official notice or website of the exam or agency, and the guides list those sources with the date they were checked. Rules change between notifications, so when a notice changes, the preset and the guide are updated. If you spot a number that is out of date, please tell us.

Built with

SlimIO stands on open-source work: pdf-lib for editing PDFs in the browser, Mozilla's PDF.js for rendering pages, JSZip for downloads with many files, and Ghostscript for server-side compression.
