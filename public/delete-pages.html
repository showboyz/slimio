How to remove pages from a PDF — in 3 steps

SlimIO's PDF page remover deletes the pages you name and keeps the rest. Everything runs in your browser, so your file stays private and there is nothing to install.

  • 1. Add your PDF. Drop in one file, or click to browse.
  • 2. List the pages to drop. Type page numbers like 1, 3, 5-7 — only the pages you list are removed.
  • 3. Download. Click Remove and save the cleaned PDF to your device.

Remove vs split vs extract

Remove deletes the pages you don't want. Extract (Split → range mode) instead keeps the pages you name. And if you want the smallest possible file, run the PDF compressor afterward.

Remove PDF pages — FAQ

How do I delete pages from a PDF for free?

Open SlimIO's PDF page remover, choose a PDF, list the pages you want to delete (e.g. 1, 3, 5-7), then click Remove. The remaining pages download as a new PDF. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account needed.

Is it safe to delete pages from a PDF online?

Yes. SlimIO edits the file locally in your browser, so your PDF never leaves your device and is not uploaded to a server.

Can I use page ranges when removing pages?

Yes. List pages like 1, 3, 5-7 and only those numbers are deleted, so every other page is kept.

Will removing pages make the file smaller?

Removing pages almost always makes the file smaller. For the smallest size, you can also use the PDF compressor afterward.

Does the free PDF page remover have a limit?
