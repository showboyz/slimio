Signature requirements on online forms

Exam, job and government application forms often ask for a scanned signature as a JPG between 10KB and 20KB, sometimes with fixed dimensions (for example 140×60 pixels or 3.5×1.5 cm). Requirements change between forms and years, so always follow the exact numbers in the official notice.

Tips for a clean signature photo

  • Use black or dark blue ink on plain white paper — no lines or grid.
  • Photograph it in daylight or under a bright lamp, straight from above, without your phone's shadow.
  • Fill most of the photo with the signature; the cleanup trims the rest.

Need a photo too?

Most forms ask for a photo alongside the signature. Use the ID photo resizer to crop it to 3×4 or 3.5×4.5 cm and fit it under the KB limit, or Sign PDF to put your signature straight onto a document.

Signature Image Maker — FAQ

How do I resize my signature to 20KB?

Draw your signature or upload a photo of it, choose Under 20KB (or 10–20KB) as the file size and click Make signature image. SlimIO trims the empty space and keeps the sharpest version that fits.

The form says the signature must be between 10KB and 20KB. Can this do that?

Yes. Choose 10–20KB. SlimIO keeps the best quality under 20KB, and if a small signature still comes out under 10KB, it adds harmless padding data to the file so it meets the minimum — the picture itself doesn't change.

How do I get a clean signature from a photo?

Sign in black or dark blue ink on plain white paper, take the photo in good light, then upload it. Clean up background turns the paper pure white (or transparent for PNG) and crops to the ink.

Can I make a signature with a transparent background?

Yes. Choose PNG — transparent background. It's ideal for placing on documents. Many upload forms only accept JPG, which always has a white background.

Is my signature uploaded anywhere?

No. Drawing, cleanup and resizing all happen in your browser. Your signature never leaves your device.
