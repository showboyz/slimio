How to rotate a PDF — in 3 steps

SlimIO's PDF rotator turns every page of a document to the orientation you want. Everything runs in your browser, so your file stays private and there is nothing to install.

  • 1. Add your PDF. Drop in one file, or click to browse.
  • 2. Pick an angle. Choose 90, 180 or 270 degrees. Every page turns the same amount.
  • 3. Download. Click Rotate and save the turned PDF to your device.

Fix a sideways PDF

Scanners and some apps save pages sideways. Rotating 90 or 270 degrees usually fixes it. To shrink the result afterward, run the PDF compressor, or use Split to keep some pages.

Rotate PDF — FAQ

How do I rotate a PDF for free?

Open SlimIO's PDF rotator, choose a PDF, pick 90, 180 or 270 degrees, and click Rotate. Every page turns and downloads as a new PDF. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account needed.

Is it safe to rotate a PDF online?

Yes. SlimIO rotates the file locally in your browser, so your PDF never leaves your device and is not uploaded to a server.

Can I rotate only some pages?

The rotator turns every page for a clean, consistent result. For per-page work, combine it with the Split tool after rotating.

Why is my PDF sideways?

Scanners and some apps save pages in landscape. Rotating 90 or 270 degrees usually fixes a sideways PDF.
