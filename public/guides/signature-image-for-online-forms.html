How to make a clean signature image for online forms (JPG 10–20KB or transparent PNG)

By SlimIO · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Online applications ask for a scanned signature more often than for almost anything else, and the rules are strict: a JPG of 10–20KB, 140 × 60 pixels, black ink, white background, no capital letters. At the same time, signing a PDF contract needs a signature without a white box around it. Both start from the same thing — a good signature on paper. This guide covers how to capture it, clean it and save it in the right format and size.

Step 1: sign on the right paper with the right pen

  • Plain white paper, no lines or grid. Ruled lines survive the cleanup and look like scratches.
  • Black pen (or dark blue where allowed). Many exam notices — RRB, SSC, IBPS — ask for black ink specifically.
  • Your normal signature, in running handwriting. Several portals reject signatures in BLOCK or CAPITAL letters.
  • Sign three times and pick the cleanest. A medium-thick pen photographs better than a fine one.

Step 2: photograph it (or draw it)

A scanner is ideal, but a phone works just as well if you avoid three problems:

  1. Shadows. Shoot from straight above in daylight or under a bright lamp, and keep your phone's shadow off the paper.
  2. Distance. Get close enough that the signature fills most of the photo. Small signatures in a large photo lose detail when they are cropped.
  3. Blur. Tap to focus on the ink and hold still; a slightly blurred signature turns grey and fuzzy when it is cleaned.

No pen and paper nearby? On the signature page you can draw with a finger or mouse. That suits documents; for exam portals that ask for a scanned signature, a photo of a real one is safer.

Step 3: clean it

Phone photos of paper are never evenly white: the middle is brighter than the corners, there are shadows, and the paper is slightly grey or yellow. Simple "remove white background" tools use one fixed brightness threshold, so either grey paper stays in the image or faint ink strokes disappear. A better approach compares each spot with the paper around it, which turns uneven paper into clean white (or transparent) while keeping thin strokes. SlimIO also removes stray specks and keeps small marks that belong to the signature, like the dot of an i.

For a form that isn't listed, paste its instructions into the Paste your form's requirements box — "Signature 140 x 60 pixels, file size between 10 KB and 20 KB, JPG" — and the pixels, range and format are set for you. If a clean signature comes out below the minimum KB, padding data is added so the file passes without changing the image. Questions

Should I upload a JPG or a PNG signature?

Upload portals almost always ask for JPG/JPEG, often with a KB range such as 10–20KB — use JPG for those. A transparent PNG is better for placing a signature on a document, because only the ink shows and the page underneath stays visible.

Can I draw my signature on the screen instead of on paper?

Yes, and for documents that is fine. Some exam notices expect a scanned signature made with a black pen on white paper; a photo of a real signature is the safest choice for those.

My signature looks grey and the paper has shadows. How do I fix it?

Photograph it in daylight from straight above so your phone doesn't cast a shadow. SlimIO's cleanup judges the paper against its surroundings, so uneven light becomes plain white and the ink stays dark.

Is a signature image legally valid?

A signature image is widely used on everyday forms, applications and quotes. It is not the same as a certificate-based digital signature. If a document must be signed with a specific e-signature method, check with whoever receives it.
