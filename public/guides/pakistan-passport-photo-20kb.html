Pakistan online passport photo: 35×45 mm, white background, under 20KB, and how to keep it sharp

By SlimIO · Updated November 8, 2026 · 7 min read

Many Pakistanis renewing a passport from abroad on the DGI&P e-Services Portal get stuck at the photo. It must be no more than 20KB, a phone photo is often 2–5MB, and squeezing it down tends to blur the face. Below are the official rules, the reason some official files say 5MB, and a way to get a sharp 20KB photo. The fingerprint form and documents come at the end.

The rules below come from DGI&P's pages and guides at onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk (checked November 2026). Apply only there: the portal warns it has no collaboration with any other website. You pay online by card, and DGI&P has a no-refund policy for any application once it is started or submitted, so prepare your files first.

20KB or 5MB: which number counts?

Both numbers appear in official DGI&P material:

  • 20KB: the home page ("Photograph up to 20KB"), the photo page ("should not exceed 20KB"), and the document upload page ("Photograph: 20KB"). The photo page's checker says "JPG / PNG max 20KB".
  • 5MB: two PDF guides still linked from the site, the Photo Capture Guide ("maximum up to 5MB") and the document upload guide ("PHOTOGRAPH: 5MB"). That photo guide also wants "professionally printed" photos with "no ink marks or creases", wording meant for paper prints.

Make your file for 20KB: every current web page says so, and a 20KB file meets a 5MB limit too, not the other way round. The portal doesn't say whether 20KB means 20,000 or 20,480 bytes, so stay under 20,000 bytes and you pass under either reading. Windows and many apps count 1KB as 1,024 bytes, so a 20,000-byte file is listed as about 19.5KB.

The photo page's checker has two error messages worth knowing: "Only Files of type JPG/PNG are allowed." means the file is something else (often an iPhone HEIC or a WebP), and "Size of the file must not be greater than 20KB." means it is too big.

Which pixel size?

No official page or guide gives a pixel size, only 35 × 45 mm and 20KB. SlimIO's Pakistan preset uses 413 × 531 pixels, which is 35 × 45 mm at 300 dpi, a standard print resolution, and it keeps the exact 35:45 shape. A larger image would need harder compression to fit 20KB; a smaller one starts with less detail.

At 531 pixels high, the 29–34 mm head rule works out to a head 342–401 pixels tall. The PDF photo guide asks for the face to fill 70–80% of the photo, so a head of about 32–33 mm (around 380 pixels) satisfies both.

"Cut down from larger pictures": what that rule means

The photo page says you can't use photos cut down from larger pictures, and that photos must not be edited in software such as Photoshop. The same page demands a file of 20KB at most, so resizing and compressing are expected. What the rule targets is cutting your face out of another picture (a group shot, a wedding photo, an old portrait) and retouching. The page also says photos must be professionally taken. Start from a fresh photo shot as a passport photo, head and shoulders against a plain white wall, then crop lightly, resize and compress. No skin smoothing, background swaps or filters.

Why 20KB looks blurry, and how to keep it sharp

A 413 × 531 photo holds about 219,000 pixels. Stored raw, that is roughly 658KB, so 20KB keeps only about 3% of the data. JPEG does this by smoothing away fine detail, and everything in the picture competes for the same 20KB.

We tested SlimIO's sample photo (a fictional person against a plain wall) and a copy with added grain, like a phone shot in a dim room. At the tool's top quality the clean photo was 20.8KB, nearly under the limit; the grainy copy was 62.9KB and had to shrink to a third to fit. Saved at 200 × 257 pixels instead, the clean photo was 10.4KB but looked soft at the same display size. As a PNG, the 413 × 531 crop was 211.9KB, over ten times the limit: PNG is lossless, so it can't get near 20KB at this size.

So, to get the clearest 20KB file:

  • Use good light. Stand facing a window in daylight. Dim light adds grain, and grain eats the 20KB budget.
  • Use a plain white wall. A flat background costs almost nothing, which leaves more of the 20KB for your face.
  • Start from the camera's original file. Screenshots and copies forwarded through messaging apps are often already shrunk.
  • Don't enlarge a small photo. Scaling it up adds pixels, not detail.
  • Save as JPG, not PNG, and keep 413 × 531 rather than shrinking the pixels.
  • Compress once. A second pass through another compressor adds blur.

Don't run the fingerprint form through SlimIO or any other compressor. The FAQ says the 600 dpi must come from the scanner setting, not from software, and re-saving can change the resolution and colour mode. If the scan is over 3MB, scan again at 600 dpi with a lower JPEG quality setting in the scanner software. The home page adds that you can now verify fingerprints in NADRA's PakID app at the fingerprint upload stage instead of using the paper form.

Keep documents readable: the portal warns that low-quality scans can slow processing. If an upload still fails, the FAQ suggests a current browser and a screen of at least 1280 × 720; the help address is mrponline@dgip.gov.pk (include your tracking ID, passport number and phone number).

Questions

What is the Pakistan passport photo size for the online application?

35 mm wide by 45 mm high, on a plain white background, as a JPG, JPEG or PNG file of no more than 20KB. The head must measure at least 29 mm and no more than 34 mm of the 45 mm height, and the photo must show your full head and shoulders and be no more than 6 months old.

What is the Pakistan passport photo size in pixels?

The passport portal's pages give no pixel size, only 35 × 45 mm and 20KB. 413 × 531 pixels is 35 × 45 mm at 300 dpi and keeps the right shape. Going much larger forces heavier compression to stay under 20KB; going much smaller leaves less detail.

What background colour is needed for a Pakistani passport photo?

Plain white. The photo page asks for a plain white background, a clear contrast between you and the background, and no shadows anywhere in the picture.

Why does my photo become blurry when I compress it to 20KB?

20KB is about 3% of the raw data in a 413 × 531 photo, so the compression has to throw away fine detail. Grain from dim light and busy backgrounds use up the same budget, and tools that shrink the pixels instead make the photo soft. Take the photo in good light against a white wall, keep 413 × 531 pixels and save it as JPG.

Is the Pakistan passport photo limit 20KB or 5MB?

Build it to 20KB. The current portal pages (home page, photo page and document upload page) all say 20KB. Two PDF guides linked from the site say 5MB. A 20KB file meets both numbers.

What size should the fingerprint form and documents be?

The fingerprint form may be up to 3MB, scanned at 600 dpi in greyscale as JPEG (2–2.5MB is called ideal). Each supporting document may be up to 1MB. All uploads must be JPEG, JPG or PNG.
