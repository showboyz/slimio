    How to convert JPG to PDF — in 3 steps

    SlimIO's JPG to PDF tool bundles your images into one PDF, in the order you choose. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay private and there is nothing to upload.

    • 1. Add your images. Drop in JPG, PNG or WebP files, or click to browse.
    • 2. Arrange the order. Use the up and down arrows to set the page order.
    • 3. Download. Click Convert and save the combined PDF to your device.

    Need a smaller PDF?

    You can also compress PDF, merge PDF, or turn a PDF into JPG — all free, all in your browser.

    How the pages are made

    Each image becomes one page of exactly the image's own size — nothing is cropped, stretched or framed with margins. JPG files are placed into the PDF as they are, without being re-compressed, so they keep their original quality; PNG files keep theirs too, and WebP images are converted to high-quality JPG first. That also means the PDF is about as big as the images you put in.

    Making a document from phone photos

    1. Photograph each page from straight above in good light, filling the frame with the paper.
    2. Crop away the desk in your phone's photo editor if needed.
    3. Add the photos here, put them in order with the arrows, and convert.
    4. If the PDF must be under a limit such as 2MB or 500KB, run it through Compress PDF or a compress-to-size page afterwards — or shrink the photos first with Compress Image.

    Tips

    • Photos straight from a modern phone are often 3–5MB each; ten of them make a 30–50MB PDF. Shrinking them first keeps the PDF light.
    • iPhone photos in HEIC format may not open in the browser. Choose "Most Compatible" under Settings → Camera → Formats, or share the photo as JPG.

    JPG to PDF — FAQ

    How do I turn several photos into one PDF?

    Add all the images at once, put them in order with the up and down arrows, and click Convert to PDF. Each image becomes one page of the PDF.

    Will the photos lose quality?

    No. JPG files are placed into the PDF without re-compression, and PNG keeps its quality too. Only WebP images are converted to high-quality JPG first.

    Why is my PDF so big?

    The PDF is about as big as the images inside it. Shrink the photos first with Compress Image, or compress the finished PDF.

    Which image types can I use?

    JPG, PNG and WebP. iPhone HEIC photos may not open in the browser — set the camera to Most Compatible or share the photos as JPG.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser; your photos never leave your device.
