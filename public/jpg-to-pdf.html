    How to convert JPG to PDF — in 3 steps

    SlimIO's JPG to PDF tool bundles your images into one PDF, in the order you choose. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay private and there is nothing to upload.

    • 1. Add your images. Drop in JPG, PNG or WebP files, or click to browse.
    • 2. Arrange the order. Use the up and down arrows to set the page order.
    • 3. Download. Click Convert and save the combined PDF to your device.
