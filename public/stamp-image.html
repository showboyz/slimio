How to make a stamp image

  • 1. Stamp it on white paper. Press the stamp firmly on plain white paper, with no lines or print under it.
  • 2. Take the photo. From straight above, in daylight or under a bright lamp, without your phone's shadow. Let the stamp fill most of the photo.
  • 3. Choose it and make the image. Pick the photo under Stamp photo and click Make stamp image. The paper becomes transparent and the image is cut to the ink.
  • 4. Download the PNG. Put it into Word, HWP or a slide, or click Stamp a PDF with it to place it on a PDF.

Clean red, keep the colour, or black

Most stamps use red seal ink. Clean red keeps only the red ink and gives it one even colour, so the stamp looks the same on screen and on paper — and printed text, pen lines and shadows around it are dropped. A blue or black rubber stamp needs Keep the photo's colour (or Black), photographed on blank paper.

No stamp? Make a name seal

Type your name under Make a name seal and pick a round or square seal. A Korean name is set top to bottom and right to left, the way name seals are carved; four letters go in two columns. It's a picture of your name, not a registered seal — use it where a simple stamp mark is enough, such as a consent form or a quote, and never in place of a registered seal.

Putting the stamp on a document

  • Word / HWP: insert the PNG as a picture, set it in front of the text, and drag it over the signature line. The transparent background lets the text show through, like a real stamp.
  • PDF: use Sign PDF — the link under the result opens it with your stamp ready to place.
  • Size: a printed stamp is usually about 1.5 cm (a name seal) to 2.5 cm (a company seal) across. Scale it to that in your document.

Only use your own stamp, or a company seal you are allowed to use. Whether a stamp image is accepted depends on who receives the document — if unsure, ask them first.

Stamp Image Maker — FAQ

How do I turn a photo of my stamp into a transparent PNG?

Stamp it on plain white paper, take a photo from straight above in good light, and choose it under Stamp photo. Click Make stamp image: the paper becomes transparent, the image is cut to the ink, and you download a PNG.

My stamp is on a document with printed text. Can I still use the photo?

Yes, if the stamp is red. Keep Ink colour on Clean red: only red ink is kept, so black print, pen lines and shadows are removed. For a blue or black stamp, choose Keep the photo's colour and photograph it on blank paper.

I don't have a stamp. Can I make one?

Open Make a name seal, type your name and pick a round or square seal. It's a picture of your name in a seal, not a registered seal, so use it only where a simple stamp mark is enough.

How do I put the stamp image into Word, HWP or a PDF?

In Word or HWP, insert the PNG as a picture and set it to sit in front of the text, then drag it onto the signature line. For a PDF, use Stamp a PDF with it under the result, which opens Sign PDF with the stamp ready.

Is my stamp uploaded anywhere?

No. The background removal and the name seal are made in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device.
