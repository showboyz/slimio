How to split a PDF — in 3 steps

SlimIO's PDF splitter breaks one document into smaller pieces. Everything runs in your browser, so your file stays private and there is nothing to install.

  • 1. Add your PDF. Drop in one file, or click to browse.
  • 2. Choose a mode. "Each page" makes every page its own PDF; "Extract a range" keeps the pages you name in a single PDF.
  • 3. Download. Click Split and save the result straight to your device.

Split vs extract

Split turns a 10-page PDF into ten one-page PDFs — handy when each page is a separate document. Extract (range mode) keeps only the pages you list, such as 1-3, 8, 10-12, inside one PDF. To shrink the result afterward, try the PDF compressor.

When to split a PDF

  • Send only what's needed. A form asks for the first page of a certificate, not all twelve — extract just that range.
  • Get under an upload limit. A 40-page scan that is too big for one upload slot can go in as two or three smaller PDFs.
  • Separate a combined scan. A scanner that saved several documents into one file can be split back into one PDF per page.

Writing page ranges

Separate pages with commas and use a dash for a range: 1-3 takes pages 1, 2 and 3; 1, 4, 7-9 takes pages 1, 4, 7, 8 and 9. The pages keep their original order and quality — they are copied, not re-rendered, so text stays selectable. To drop a few pages and keep the rest, Remove pages is quicker.

PDF splitter — FAQ

How do I split a PDF for free?

Open SlimIO's PDF splitter, choose a PDF, pick "Each page as a separate PDF" or a page range like 1-3, 5, then click Split. The result downloads as a ZIP of pages or a single PDF.

Can I extract only some pages from a PDF?

Yes. In range mode you can type pages like 1-3, 8, 10-12 and only those pages are kept in the new PDF.

Is it safe to split a PDF online?

Yes. SlimIO splits files locally in your browser, so your PDF never leaves your device and is not uploaded to a server.

What is the difference between split and extract?

Split makes every page its own one-page PDF. Extract keeps a chosen range as a single multi-page PDF.

Does the free PDF splitter have a limit?

The free tier includes a per-day operation limit. No signup is required, and you can split right away.
