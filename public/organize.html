How to reorder PDF pages — in 3 steps

SlimIO's Organize PDF tool shows a preview of every page so you can see exactly what you are moving. Everything runs in your browser, so your file stays private and there is nothing to install.

  • 1. Add your PDF. Drop in one file, or click to browse. Each page appears as a thumbnail.
  • 2. Arrange the pages. Drag pages into the right order, or tap ← and →. Rotate a single page with ↻, or remove it with ✕.
  • 3. Save. Click Save PDF and download the reorganized file.

Fix scans that came out of order

Double-sided scans and merged files often end up with pages in the wrong order or upside down. Put them right here, then add page numbers or run the PDF compressor. To combine several files first, use Merge PDF.

When to use it

  • Fix a scan order. Double-sided scans often come out as all fronts and then all backs; drag the pages back into reading order.
  • Tidy a merged file. After merging several PDFs, move the cover to the front and drop blank pages.
  • Turn single pages. Rotate just the landscape pages in a portrait document.

What happens to quality

The thumbnails are only for arranging. When you save, the original pages are copied into the new PDF in your order, with your rotations — they are not re-rendered, so text stays selectable and images keep their quality.

Organize PDF — FAQ

How do I rearrange pages in a PDF for free?

Open SlimIO's Organize PDF tool and choose a PDF. Every page shows as a preview. Drag pages into the order you want, or use the arrow buttons, then click Save PDF.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Click the rotate button under any page to turn only that page 90 degrees clockwise. Other pages stay as they are.

Can I delete pages while reordering?

Yes. Click the delete button under a page to drop it from the saved PDF.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. On touch screens, use the left and right arrow buttons under each page to move it.

Is it safe to organize a PDF online?

Yes. SlimIO reorders the pages locally in your browser, so your PDF never leaves your device and is not uploaded to a server.
