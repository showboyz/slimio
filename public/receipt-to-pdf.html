    How to turn receipts into one PDF — in 4 steps

    Expense reports, research budgets and tax filings often ask for the receipts as a single PDF, sometimes with several receipts on each A4 sheet. SlimIO lays the pages out for you, in your browser, so the receipts are never uploaded.

    • 1. Add the receipt photos. Drop in JPG, PNG, WebP or iPhone HEIC files, or click to browse.
    • 2. Put them in order. Use the arrows, or sort by file name or file date.
    • 3. Choose the layout. Pick 1, 2, 4 or 6 receipts per page, numbering, a cover page, and a size limit if the form has one.
    • 4. Make the PDF. Download it and attach it to your report or upload form.

    Several receipts per A4 page

    Many offices want receipts attached to A4 sheets rather than one photo per page. Pick 2, 4 or 6 per page and SlimIO chooses the arrangement that draws the receipts largest: long, narrow till receipts stand side by side, wide ones go one above the other. Each photo is first cut down to the receipt itself, so the table around it doesn't take up the space. Each receipt gets a thin frame and, if you like, a number in the corner.

    A cover page with a list and total

    Tick Add a cover page and type the date, store and amount for each receipt. The first page becomes a table whose numbers match the numbers on the receipts, with the total at the bottom. SlimIO does not read the receipts automatically, so you decide exactly what the table says.

    Under 5MB, 10MB or 20MB

    Phone photos are several megabytes each, so a stack of receipts quickly passes an upload limit. Choose the limit under PDF size limit, or type any size with Custom limit. The pages are saved again at a lower JPG quality first and then at a smaller size until the whole PDF fits, and the result tells you what was done. If it still cannot fit, it says so and gives the smallest file it could make.

    Clearer faded receipts

    Thermal paper fades to light grey. Make faded text clearer turns each receipt to black and white and stretches its brightness so the print is dark and the paper white. Photograph the receipt flat, from straight above, in even light, and keep your finger and shadows out of the picture.

    Tips

    • Photograph receipts on a darker surface: each one is then cut out of its photo automatically. On a white table, crop the photo in your phone's editor first — the larger the receipt fills its photo, the larger it prints.
    • Name the photos in order, such as 01, 02, 03, and use Sort by file name.
    • iPhone HEIC photos copied to a computer work as they are — no need to convert them to JPG first.
    • Need to merge PDFs you already have? Use Merge PDF, or compress a PDF that is too large.

    Receipts to PDF — FAQ

    How do I put several receipts on one A4 page?

    Add the receipt photos, choose how many per page (2, 4 or 6) and click Make PDF. Each page is laid out so the receipts are as large as possible — tall receipts go side by side, wide ones one above the other.

    Can the PDF have a cover page with a list and total?

    Yes. Tick Add a cover page, fill in the date, store and amount for each receipt, and the first page lists them with numbers that match the numbers on the receipts, plus the total.

    How do I get the PDF under 5MB or 10MB?

    Pick the limit under PDF size limit before you click Make PDF. The pages are saved again at a lower quality and then a smaller size until the whole PDF fits. Any other size works with Custom limit.

    My receipt text is faded. Can it be made clearer?

    Tick Make faded text clearer. Each receipt is turned to black and white and its levels are stretched, so grey thermal-paper print turns dark on white. It works best on photos taken straight on, in good light.

    Can it cut the table out of my receipt photos?

    Yes. Cut the receipt out of each photo is on by default: SlimIO finds the paper against a darker table, straightens a slightly crooked receipt and drops the rest, so the receipt prints larger. On a white table it can't tell paper from table and leaves the photo as it is — put the receipt on something darker, or crop the photo first.

    In what order do the receipts go?

    In the order of the list. Use the arrows to move one, or Sort by file name or Sort by file date to order them all at once.

    Which image types can I use?

    JPG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos. HEIC photos are opened in your browser too; the first one takes a few seconds longer while the decoder loads.

    Are my receipts uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser; your receipts never leave your device.
