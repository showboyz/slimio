    How to merge PDF files online — in 3 steps

    SlimIO's PDF merger joins two or more PDF documents into a single file. Everything runs in your browser, so your documents stay private and there is nothing to install.

    • 1. Add your PDFs. Drop in two or more PDF files, or click to browse and select several at once.
    • 2. Arrange the order. Use the up and down arrows to control which pages come first.
    • 3. Download. Click Merge and save the combined PDF straight to your device.

    Why merge PDFs?

    PDF merger — FAQ

    How do I merge PDF files for free?

    Open SlimIO's PDF merger, drop in two or more PDF files, reorder them if you like, and click Merge. The result downloads as a single combined PDF. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account needed.

    Yes. SlimIO merges files locally in your browser, so your PDFs never leave your device and are not uploaded to any server.

    You can merge as many PDF files as your browser memory allows. For very large batches, split the work into a few passes.
